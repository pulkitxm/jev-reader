The quiet art of
starting again.

A SAMPLE ESSAY · 4 MIN READ

There is a particular frustration in knowing what you want to say, but not quite finding the words.

I used to think that good writing had to sound pretentious. Every sentence needed to prove something. The result was an overwhelming collection of ideas that nobody wanted to read.

A smaller, kinder beginning

Then a friend offered a different perspective: write as if you are talking to someone you care about. Be curious. Stay patient. Make room for uncertainty.

Progress is rarely obvious. Sometimes it is simply the willingness to try again, with a little more compassion than before.
